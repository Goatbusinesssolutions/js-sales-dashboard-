How This Dashboard Works

Sales totals, pacing, targets, and appointment resulting & tags — in plain language.

Sales totals

Every dollar figure on the main dashboard is a won opportunity in GOAT, across every pipeline, counted by its Won Date — not when the estimate was given, not when the deal was created.

Won Date is read from the moment GOAT automatically flips an opportunity's status to "won" (an instant, automatic event the moment a contract is signed — no rep, no manual entry, no lag), converted into a calendar date in the business's own timezone so a contract signed at 9:56pm ET isn't misfiled onto the next day. A separate, manually-maintained "Won Date" custom field is used only as a fallback for the rare record missing that automatic timestamp.

A week runs Sunday through Saturday. A working day (used only for pacing math below, never for the dollar totals themselves) is Monday through Saturday — a sale that closes on a Sunday still counts in every dollar total, it just isn't counted in the day denominator.

Pacing & projections

The projected total shown for a month or year is a straight-line run-rate: average $ per working day × working days in the period. That average is deliberately computed through yesterday only — today is still in progress, so treating its partial revenue as a full day's would understate the pace and make things look worse than they are. It is a projection, not a target.

The behind pace / on pace callouts and the "$X,XXX/working day" catch-up figure answer a different question: what's needed for the rest of the period to still land on target by period end — not a flat average spread over the whole period.

Targets

The current weekly sales target is $300,000, effective the week of October 4, 2026 forward (it was $288,000 before that — see the changelog).

Monthly and yearly targets are blended across that cutover: working days before Oct 4 are still measured against the old $288k/week rate, working days from Oct 4 onward against the new $300k/week rate, and the two are summed. That keeps a month or year that straddles the raise from being retroactively judged against a number that wasn't the goal yet for its earlier days.

Appointment outcomes — how "resulting" works

Every booked Estimate Calendar appointment that has already happened gets sorted into exactly one outcome bucket, per rules confirmed with the business:

Sold
The linked deal's status is won. The appointment itself is tagged Sold on the calendar, but the dollar figure attached to it is always sourced by Won Date — the same basis as the sales totals above — so the two never disagree for the same date range.
DNS
"Did Not Sell (yet)" — an estimate was given, no decision yet. The linked deal is open and sitting in one of three stages: Rep Working, Rehash (Leads pipeline), or Estimate Sent (Leads Commercial pipeline).
No Show
The calendar marks the appointment noshow. Folds in "One Leg" too — GOAT doesn't track that as a separate status, and both mean the same thing for counting purposes (doesn't count as an opportunity).
Inc. Time
"Inconvenient Time" — the calendar marks it invalid and the contact carries an "inc time" tag. The full buying committee was present, but no demo/price was given (someone was tied up, unwell, etc.). Unlike No Show, this does count as an opportunity in the rate math below, because a decision-maker was actually in the room. The deal itself stays open and just moves to the Reset stage.
Not Interested (NI)
The linked deal's status is lost (for appointments on/after Aug 16, 2026) — or, before that date, or when there's no linked deal at all, a "not interested"-family tag on the contact.
Not Qualified (NQ)
The linked deal's status is abandoned (for appointments on/after Aug 16, 2026) — or, before that date, or when there's no linked deal at all, a "not qualified"-family tag.
Unresulted
Showed up on the calendar but doesn't fit any bucket above yet. This is a data-quality flag, not a business outcome — it almost always means the linked deal is open but parked in a stage that isn't one of the three DNS stages above (commonly Reset). The drill-down list on the dashboard names the exact stage and what it needs to move to (Rep Working/Rehash, Won, Lost, or Abandoned), with a link straight to the contact in GOAT to fix it there.
Excluded
Calendar status cancelled, or invalid with none of the Inc. Time / NI / NQ signals above.
Upcoming
Status confirmed or new with a start time still in the future — hasn't happened yet.
Seeing an appointment stuck as Unresulted? Nine times out of ten the fix isn't on the dashboard — it's in GOAT. Open the contact from the drill-down link and move the linked deal out of whatever holding stage it's in (Reset, most often) into Rep Working/Rehash, Won, Lost, or Abandoned. The dashboard re-pulls appointment data every couple of minutes in the background, so the fix shows up here shortly after you make it in GOAT — no need to refresh repeatedly.

Why dates before Aug 16, 2026 read a little differently

Before Aug 16, 2026, the automation that applies NI/NQ tags deleted the linked opportunity instead of changing its status — so for appointments before that date, the opportunity-status signal is incomplete, and the dashboard falls back to the tag on the contact instead. Sold, DNS, and No Show are unaffected and fully reliable at any date.

Any date range that touches a date before Aug 16, 2026 automatically switches the rate math into Modified mode (you'll see a badge on the appointment-outcomes section showing which mode is active), which drops NI/NQ from the formulas entirely rather than report an unreliable number.
