$ sold = total value of that rep's won deals by Won Date (see the note above the counts) — attributed to the opportunity's own owner, not necessarily whoever ran the original estimate appointment; usually the same person, occasionally not. Appointments = every resulted appointment for that rep (Sold + DNS + No Show + Inc. Estimates sent = Sold + DNS (both mean a price was actually given). Close rate = Sold ÷ Estimates sent in both modes, so it always divides out exactly. Demo rate (Estimates sent ÷ Opportunities) also counts Inc.
