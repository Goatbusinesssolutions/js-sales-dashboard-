Changelog

What's changed on this dashboard, and why — newest first.
October 5, 2026
Appointment outcomes section still loading slow
The problem
The appointment-outcomes section's edge cache lasted exactly 120 seconds — the same length as the automatic background refresh that keeps it pre-warmed. Any refresh that landed even a few seconds late, or occasionally failed, meant the cache had already gone cold by the time a real visitor showed up, and they paid for a live pull from GOAT themselves — which, for this section specifically, can take up to about a minute.
What we changed
Widened the cache window from 120 seconds to 180 seconds — a full extra refresh cycle of buffer past the 2-minute warm-up schedule.
Why it matters
A late or occasionally-skipped background refresh no longer means a visitor hits the slow live-pull path. Trade-off: a status change made in GOAT can now take up to 180 seconds to show up here instead of 120 — worth it for making the slow path rare instead of routine.
October 5, 2026
Weekly sales target raised to $300,000
The problem
The business raised its weekly sales target from $288,000 to $300,000, effective the week of October 4, 2026 — but the dashboard's monthly and yearly targets are derived from the weekly number, and a flat change would have retroactively judged September and early-October days against a target that didn't exist yet when those days happened.
What we changed
The weekly target now shows $300,000. Monthly and yearly targets are "blended" across the October 4 cutover: working days before that date are still measured against the old $288,000/week rate, working days from that date forward are measured against the new $300,000/week rate, and the two are summed for whatever period is being shown.
Why it matters
Progress bars and pacing stay fair across the change — nobody is measured against a goal that wasn't the goal yet when they were hitting the numbers that count toward it. This also means no further changes are needed once October and 2026 fully pass the cutover; the math naturally reduces to a flat $300,000/week target from November 2026 onward.
October 5, 2026
Main dashboard loading slow
The problem
Same root cause as the appointment-outcomes fix above, but for the main sales dashboard's data: a 120-second cache window exactly matching the 2-minute background refresh cycle left no margin for a late or skipped tick.
What we changed
Widened the cache window from 120 seconds to 150 seconds.
Why it matters
Far fewer live, multi-second pulls for a real visitor — most page loads now come straight from an already-warm cache.
August 27, 2026
Appointment outcomes erroring out / rate-limited
The problem
The appointment-outcomes endpoint pulls three separate, heavy paginated data sets from GOAT: every opportunity, every tagged contact, and every calendar event in range. Running those three pulls at the same time — even though each one paced its own requests internally — added their request rates together and tripped GOAT's shared rate limit for this account, causing errors.
What we changed
The three pulls now run one after another instead of all at once. The two background cache-warming schedules (one for the main dashboard, one for appointment outcomes) were also moved onto alternating minutes instead of both firing on the same minute, so they stop colliding with each other too.
Why it matters
No more rate-limit errors on this section. Trade-off: a cold pull (cache completely empty) now takes up to about a minute end-to-end instead of a few seconds — but a real visitor almost never pays that cost directly, since the background refresh keeps the cache warm ahead of time in the overwhelming majority of cases.
This log covers the changes that affect what you see or how numbers are calculated — not every small code or styling commit. For how the dashboard calculates and classifies things day to day, see How This Dashboard Works.
